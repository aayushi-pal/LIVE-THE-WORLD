Package Overview

Slow mornings, pine-scented walks, and the gentle rhythm of the valley. This handcrafted escape combines time to explore with room to unwind. Your final route, accommodation, and travel arrangements can be tailored before confirmation.

  • Discover Parvati Valley at a comfortable, unhurried pace
  • Make room for local walks, riverside cafes, and scenic stops
  • Choose your vehicle and driver preference from our fleet
  • Discuss personal interests, photography pauses, and route changes

Day Wise Itinerary

DAY 01 Arrival & Riverside Evening
Arrive in the valley, check in to your riverside retreat, and settle into the serene mountain rhythm. Enjoy a relaxed riverside walk along the Parvati river where access and weather conditions allow. Unwind by the evening hearth.
DAY 02 Valley Walks & Local Discovery
Explore local pine trails, charming village lanes, artisan cafés, and scenic viewpoints at your own pace. Discuss optional guided nature walks or visits to Manikaran natural hot springs before travel.
DAY 03 A Slow Morning & Departure
Enjoy an artisan breakfast overlooking the mist-clad mountain ridges and take a final peaceful mountain pause before checking out for your onward journey.
