Shimla - Queen of the Hills

The historic summer capital of British India, perched along pine-clad ridges. Stroll along the pedestrian-only Mall Road, visit the neo-Gothic Christ Church, and explore apple orchards in Mashobra.

  • Historic Ridge & British Architecture
  • Jakhu Temple with Panoramic Mountain Views
  • Kufri Snow Viewpoint & Horse Riding
  • Chail Wildlife Sanctuary & Apple Belt

Manali & Solang Valley

Nestled in the Beas River Valley, Manali is India's adventure capital. Soar above Solang Valley with certified tandem paragliding pilots, conquer the snowy passes of Rohtang at 13,050 ft, and relax in Old Manali cafes.

  • High-Altitude Paragliding at Solang Valley
  • Rohtang Pass (13,050 ft) Snow Point & Atal Tunnel
  • White-Water River Rafting in Beas Rapids
  • Ancient Hadimba Temple & Vashisht Sulfur Springs

Parvati Valley & Kasol

A dramatic gorge carved by the torrential Parvati River, renowned for mystical pine forests, peaceful wooden villages like Tosh, natural healing hot water springs in Manikaran, and cozy riverside dome camps.

  • Sacred Natural Hot Sulfur Springs at Manikaran
  • Riverside Luxury Camping with Evening Bonfires
  • Tosh Village & Chalal Pine Forest Trail
  • Kheerganga Alpine Meadow & Glacier Views
